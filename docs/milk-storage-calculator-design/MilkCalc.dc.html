NHS storage guidance

Fridge Up to 8 days at 4°C or colder, at the back, not the door
Ice compartment Up to 2 weeks
Freezer Up to 6 months at −18°C or colder
Defrosted Use it straight away. Never refreeze it.
Left after a feed Throw it away
