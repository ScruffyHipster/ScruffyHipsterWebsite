Questions

Where do the dates come from?

From the NHS guidance on storing breast milk: up to 8 days in a fridge at 4°C or colder, up to 2 weeks in the fridge’s ice compartment, and up to 6 months in a freezer at −18°C or colder.

What if I’m not sure how cold my fridge is?

The 8 days assume 4°C or colder. If you can’t be sure, ask your midwife, health visitor or GP what’s right for you.

How long is defrosted milk good for?

The NHS says to use defrosted milk straight away and never refreeze it. Thawing it slowly in the fridge is best, and it should never be thawed in a microwave.

Does this work for formula?

No. These dates are only for expressed breast milk. Formula has its own guidance on the pack and from the NHS.

Is it free?

Yes. There’s nothing to sign up for and nothing to install.

Storage dates are guidance, not a guarantee, and this calculator isn’t medical advice. If your baby is in hospital, premature or unwell, follow their care team’s advice on storing milk.
