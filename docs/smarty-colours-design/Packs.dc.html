How to print a pack

It takes about a minute, and there is nothing to sign up for.

  1. 1

    Pick a pack

    Choose the one your child loves most. Each pack has its own page of pictures.

  2. 2

    Download the PDF

    Get all five free pages in one file, or one page at a time, sized for A4 or US Letter.

  3. 3

    Print and colour

    Black and white pages print well on any home printer. Pens, crayons or paint all work.
