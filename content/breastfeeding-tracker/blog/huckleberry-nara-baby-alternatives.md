# The short answer

The best Huckleberry or Nara Baby alternative depends on the part of the app you were actually using.

| If your priority is… | Start with… | Why it may fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Breastfeeding, bottles, pumping and stored milk | Breastfeeding Tracker & Timer | Focused feeding tools, individual fridge and freezer portions, Apple Watch timers and no subscription |
| Nap planning and sleep schedules | Napper | Sleep-led schedules, analysis, sounds and tracking |
| A detailed record shared across iPhone, Android and the web | Baby Connect | Broad care logs, caregiver sync, reports and cross-platform access |
| Broad baby tracking with a free core | Baby Tracker – Newborn Log | Feeding, sleep, nappies, health and milestones across iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch |

**Our pick for feeding is [Breastfeeding Tracker & Timer](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6754637800?pt=120062009&ct=seo_breast_bottle_pumping&mt=8).** Its free tools cover breastfeeding, bottles, pumping, combined feeds and milk storage. A one-time Lifetime Unlock adds full history, broader insights, a weekly timeline, PDF export, every theme and an ad-free experience.

It is not a like-for-like replacement for every Huckleberry or Nara feature. It does not track sleep, nappies or growth, and it does not currently offer a shared caregiver account. If those are the records you rely on, one of the broader alternatives below will make more sense.

# Why look for a Huckleberry alternative?

Huckleberry remains a capable all-round baby tracker. Its free tier records sleep, feeding, nappies, pumping, growth and more, with reports and caregiver syncing. Its distinguishing sleep tools sit in paid plans: Plus includes SweetSpot nap and bedtime predictions, Schedule Creator, data-led Insights and enhanced reports, while Premium adds personalised sleep plans and further guidance.

That makes the decision fairly clear. If SweetSpot is the reason you use Huckleberry, a simple feeding timer is not an equivalent replacement. A sleep-led app such as Napper is the closer comparison. If you mostly open Huckleberry to record feeds, however, a smaller app may remove features and subscription prompts you do not need.

[Huckleberry’s current feature pages](https://huckleberrycare.com/product/free) confirm that basic logging remains free, so you do not have to leave simply because you do not want a paid sleep plan. Compare the workflow as well as the price.

# Why look for a Nara Baby alternative?

Nara Baby is designed as a shared hub for feeding, pumping, sleep, nappies, growth, routines, pregnancy and postpartum records. Its calm design and caregiver access are central strengths.

Its payment model has changed. Nara’s current FAQ describes a seven-day trial followed by a monthly or lifetime plan. One payment covers the caregiver group, and both plans include the full feature set. If a family does not subscribe after the trial, existing records remain available to view and export, but new activities cannot be added.

That change makes “Nara Baby alternative” a different search from “best baby tracker”. Some families want another shared all-round record. Others discover that they only need feeding timers or a quick answer to “which side did I use last?”. The second group can choose a much more focused app.

# Best feeding-focused alternative: Breastfeeding Tracker & Timer

Breastfeeding Tracker & Timer is built around the feeding journey rather than the whole nursery. You can time left and right breastfeeding, record bottles and pumping, link a breastfeed and bottle top-up as one Combined Feed, and correct a missed or inaccurate entry later.

The free milk store keeps individual portions in the fridge or freezer, including the amount remaining. Pumping records can add expressed milk and bottles of expressed milk can reduce it, so the inventory sits alongside the feeding history rather than in a separate app.

Large controls help when you have one hand free. Live Activities keep an active timer visible on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, while the Apple Watch app can start and stop breastfeeding or pumping without reaching for your phone. Recent feeding information remains quick to reach; the optional Lifetime Unlock adds the longer view.

**It is a particularly good alternative if:**

- feeding is the main thing you record;
- you combine breastfeeding with bottles or pumping;
- you want to track expressed milk by fridge and freezer portion;
- you prefer a one-time upgrade to another subscription;
- you use an iPhone and Apple Watch.

**Look elsewhere if** you need Android access, live caregiver sharing, sleep prediction, nappy logs or growth records. That narrower scope is deliberate, but it will not suit every household.

[Try Breastfeeding Tracker & Timer](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6754637800?pt=120062009&ct=seo_breast_bottle_pumping&mt=8), or see how it handles [breastfeeding, bottles and pumping together](/breastfeeding-tracker/support/breastfeeding-bottle-pumping-tracker/) and [fridge and freezer milk storage](/breastfeeding-tracker/).

# Best sleep-led alternative: Napper

Napper is the more relevant alternative when Huckleberry’s sleep support, rather than its feeding log, attracted you. Its official plan page lists daily sleep tracking, age-based schedules, sleep analysis and personalisation, a sleep course, sounds, trends and statistics. Paid access is subscription-based, and availability or price can vary by store and region.

This is still not a direct copy of Huckleberry. The prediction method, guidance and interface differ, and no app can promise how an individual baby will sleep. Trial it with your real routine before moving months of habits around it.

Choose Napper when sleep planning is the job you want the app to do. Choose a broader tracker if feeds, nappies, medicines and caregiver handovers matter just as much.

# Best detailed shared record: Baby Connect

Baby Connect is worth considering when Nara’s shared family record is the feature you need to replace. It covers feeding, sleep, nappies, pumping, growth, medicines, vaccines, milestones and other activities. Caregivers can add and view records, and the service is available on iPhone, Android, Apple Watch and the web.

Its strength is breadth. Reports, charts and web access can suit families who want a detailed record across several device types, or who need to coordinate with a nanny or childcare provider. The current App Store listing describes a seven-day trial followed by a family subscription for continued entry creation.

That depth may feel heavier than Nara’s calmer interface or a dedicated feeding app. It is a better match for a household that values a comprehensive shared record over minimal setup.

# Best broad tracker with a free core: Baby Tracker – Newborn Log

Baby Tracker – Newborn Log by Nighp Software records breastfeeding, bottles, pumping, sleep, nappies, growth, health, medicines, milestones and notes. Its App Store listing says core tracking remains free, with syncing across devices, caregiver sharing, widgets, Live Activities, Apple Watch and PDF reports.

The free app contains advertising. Its optional Baby Tracker Plus subscription removes ads and adds personalised “What’s Next” timing cues for sleep, feeds and nappies. That makes the core tracker a credible starting point for someone leaving a paid plan, while keeping the distinction between free recording and paid predictions clear.

It is available on Apple platforms rather than Android. If mixed-device access is essential, Baby Connect is the stronger candidate from this shortlist.

# Compare the trade-offs before switching

| App | Strongest fit | Access approach | Main trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Breastfeeding Tracker & Timer | Feeding and expressed-milk storage | Free core; optional one-time Lifetime Unlock | iPhone and Apple Watch; no sleep, nappies or caregiver sync |
| Napper | Sleep schedules and nap planning | Free download with paid sleep plan | Sleep-led rather than a full family care record |
| Baby Connect | Detailed multi-caregiver tracking | Trial, then subscription for continued logging | More breadth and setup than a focused tracker |
| Baby Tracker – Newborn Log | General Apple-device tracking | Free core with ads; optional Plus subscription | No Android app; advanced timing cues are paid |
| Huckleberry | Broad tracking plus paid sleep prediction | Free tracker; Plus and Premium subscriptions | Its defining sleep tools require a paid plan |
| Nara Baby | Calm shared tracking across baby and parent care | Trial, then monthly or lifetime access | New logging becomes read-only without a plan after the trial |

No row is a universal winner. Decide which two or three actions must be effortless at 3 a.m. A feature you never record should not outweigh the timer, handover or report you use every day.

# How to change baby tracking apps without losing the useful history

Before cancelling anything, export a copy of your data. Huckleberry provides a CSV export from the child profile, and Nara offers CSV export from the Activity screen. Keep that file even if your replacement cannot import it; it preserves the record you may want for your own reference.

Breastfeeding Tracker & Timer does not currently import Huckleberry or Nara history. A practical switch is to keep the old export, choose a clean start date and run both apps for a day or two while you learn the new controls. Avoid maintaining two full logs for weeks unless it genuinely helps—the purpose of a tracker is to reduce mental load.

Check these points with every shortlist:

- Does it run on every phone or watch your household needs?
- Can each caregiver add records, or only view them?
- Which features remain usable without payment?
- Is the export useful outside the app?
- Can you correct a forgotten timer without distorting the day?
- Does the app help you feel organised, or make you feel watched by the data?

# FAQs

## What is the best Huckleberry alternative for breastfeeding?

Our pick is Breastfeeding Tracker & Timer when breastfeeding, bottles, pumping and expressed-milk storage are the priority. Core tracking and milk storage are free, and the optional Lifetime Unlock is a one-time purchase. It does not replace Huckleberry’s sleep predictions or broad baby-care log.

## What is the best Nara Baby alternative for shared care?

Baby Connect is the closest option in this shortlist for a detailed record shared across caregivers, iPhone, Android and the web. Baby Tracker – Newborn Log also supports caregiver syncing on Apple devices. Breastfeeding Tracker & Timer does not currently offer caregiver sharing.

## Is Nara Baby still free?

Nara’s official FAQ currently describes a seven-day free trial, followed by monthly or lifetime access. Without a plan, existing records remain available to view and export, but the family cannot add new activities.

## Is there a free Huckleberry alternative?

Huckleberry itself still includes free basic tracking. Baby Tracker – Newborn Log also keeps its core tracker free with advertising, while Breastfeeding Tracker & Timer includes free feeding and milk-storage tools. Paid features and regional terms can change, so check the current store page before choosing.

## Can I transfer my Huckleberry or Nara history to another app?

Both services provide CSV export, but an export file does not guarantee that another app can import it. Breastfeeding Tracker & Timer does not currently import either history. Save the export for reference and confirm import support with the replacement app before cancelling.
