# Record the side when the timer starts

Breastfeeding Tracker & Timer begins with two clear choices: left and right. Selecting a side starts the timer and associates that choice with the session.

When you stop, the feed is saved with its start time, duration, and selected side. The latest history remains visible so you can check what you previously recorded without trying to reconstruct the night from memory.

# Check the last recorded side

The app highlights the most recently logged side in the main view and its widget. This is a record of your selection, not a recommendation about which side to use next.

If you switch sides during a feed, use the controls available in the active session so the saved entry reflects what you intended to record.

# Correct the history when needed

Tracking is only useful if it can tolerate ordinary interruptions. From history you can:

- Add a feed that was not timed.
- Edit an existing entry.
- Delete an entry you no longer want.
- Adjust a timer that was started late.

These controls help the history match what you remember without turning every session into administrative work.

# See the same record across iPhone and Watch

Starting or stopping from Apple Watch sends the completed session back to iPhone. When a device is temporarily offline, the watch can keep the session and sync it after the connection returns.

The iPhone app is the main place for reviewing and correcting history, while the watch is optimized for fast controls.

# Keep side tracking in perspective

The app records the side you chose. It does not assess feeding adequacy, recommend a side, or provide medical advice. If you have questions about feeding or your baby's health, use guidance from an appropriate healthcare professional rather than an app history.
