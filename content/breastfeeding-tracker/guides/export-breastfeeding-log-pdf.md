# Review the history first

Before exporting, scan the saved feed history and correct anything you want the document to reflect. Breastfeeding Tracker & Timer lets you add missing feeds, edit existing entries, delete unwanted entries, and adjust a timer that was started late.

A PDF is a snapshot. Changes made inside the app after exporting do not alter a file that has already been saved.

# Choose the history to export

Open the improved history view and select the date range or entries available for export. Create the PDF, preview it, and confirm that it contains the record you intended to include.

Use the iOS share sheet to save the file or send it to a destination you choose.

# Treat the PDF as personal information

The app's current App Store privacy label states that the developer does not collect data. Exporting is different: once a PDF is saved to another service or shared with another person, that destination controls its own copy.

Check the recipient and storage location before sending a feeding history. Delete extra exported copies when you no longer need them.

# What the export represents

The document reflects the information entered in the app, such as recorded times, durations, and sides. It is not a clinical report, and the app does not verify that the history is complete or medically significant.

If someone has asked you for particular feeding information, confirm what they actually need rather than assuming the PDF replaces a conversation or professional assessment.

# Keep the source history editable

The iPhone history remains the working record. Use it to correct entries and create a fresh PDF whenever you need an updated snapshot.
