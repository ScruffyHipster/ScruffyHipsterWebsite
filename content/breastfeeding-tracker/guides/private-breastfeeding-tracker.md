# Start without creating an account

Breastfeeding Tracker & Timer does not require an account before you can start recording feeds. There is no profile form, social feed, or advertising setup between opening the app and starting the timer.

That matters because a feeding history can feel personal even when it only contains times, durations, and the side selected.

# What the App Store privacy label says

The current App Store listing states “Data Not Collected.” Apple explains that privacy labels reflect the developer's declared data practices, and the listing links to the full Scruffy Hipster privacy policy.

The app is not designed to sell feeding information, build an advertising profile, or track activity across other companies' apps and websites.

# Private summaries on your device

The app can create summaries from recent history, such as average recorded duration, the longest recorded session, left/right balance, and intervals between entries. Those summaries are processed on the device and are informational only.

They do not diagnose feeding issues, assess milk supply, or tell you whether a pattern is healthy.

# Be deliberate when exporting

A PDF export is useful when you want a portable copy of selected history. Once a PDF leaves the app, its privacy depends on where you save or send it. Review the destination before sharing personal information.

You remain in control of editing and deleting entries in the app. Uninstalling the app may also remove locally stored information, subject to the storage and synchronization behaviour described in the current privacy policy.

# A focused tool rather than a social platform

The product is designed around a timer, history, and quick device controls. It does not need community features or an account system to perform those jobs.
