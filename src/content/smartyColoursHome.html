Calm, safe colouring for little ones

SmartyColours is made for toddlers, preschoolers and young children. There is nothing to buy on this site, nothing to sign up for, and nothing that follows your child around the web.

  • No ads, ever

    Nothing pops up and nothing tries to sell to your child.

  • Grown-up gate

    Links that leave the site ask a grown-up a quick question first.

  • No sign-up

    No accounts, names or email. Pictures stay on your device.

  • Free printables

    Five free pages from every pack. Print a whole pack or pick one favourite.

Questions from grown-ups

Something else on your mind? Use the contact link at the bottom of the page.

Are the printable packs available?

Yes. Every pack has five free pages. Download the whole pack or a single sheet as an A4 or US Letter PDF, or colour any page online.

Do we need to sign up?

No. There are no accounts and nothing to fill in. Open a picture and start colouring.

Does colouring online save anything about my child?

No. Colouring stays in this browser tab. Save picture downloads a PNG to your device, and Share asks a grown-up first. There are no names, accounts or artwork uploads.

What age is it for?

The pictures are drawn for toddlers, preschoolers and young children, with big shapes and thick lines.

What do we get in the app?

Hundreds of pages across themed packs, pinch-to-zoom, and Apple Pencil support on iPad. It is free to try, with optional in-app purchases for more content.
